09 // ABOUT THE BUILDER

Not a random collection of links.

BCHtools.cash represents years of experimentation and ongoing work with Bitcoin Cash — told through the projects themselves.

alberdioni8406

Independent developer · BCH tools for the ecosystem

BCHtools is independently built and maintained for the Bitcoin Cash ecosystem. The tools are free to use. Keeping them online, maintained and improved requires infrastructure and development time — community support makes that possible.

Every project in this archive follows the same philosophy: build something real, be honest about what stage it's actually at, and keep building instead of chasing a single launch moment. Some tools are finished. Some are still in the lab. None of them are pretending to be further along than they are.
