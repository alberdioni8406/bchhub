Built independently.

BCHtools.cash is not a company product, not a charity site, and not a personal portfolio alone. It is a practical toolkit that depends on community support to stay online, maintained, and improved.

THE WORK

Years of experimentation

The projects on this site are connected by a single thread: independent building for Bitcoin Cash. Some tools are finished and in daily use. Some are still in the lab. Some need one more push to land on a permanent domain.

Most of the software is open source. This site is too. Fork it, fix the copy, add a tool. Inspect the code. Criticize it. Suggest improvements.
