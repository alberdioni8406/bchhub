05 // FUND ME TOOLS

No deadline.
The work does not expire.

Tools don't wait for a fundraising campaign to start working. They need maintenance while nobody is watching. They break at inconvenient times. APIs change. Domains expire. New ideas appear at 2 AM.

And yes — sometimes the person maintaining them needs a few coins for hosting… and perhaps a Red Bull to stay awake and keep building.

This is not a crowdfunding campaign. There is no expiration date. There is no “if we don’t reach the goal, nothing happens.” Support is always open because the work is always ongoing.
