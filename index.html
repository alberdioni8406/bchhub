WHY SUPPORT MATTERS

Tools do not stay online by magic

Domains expire. Hosting costs money. APIs change. Bugs appear at inconvenient times. Development takes time — and occasionally a Red Bull.

Building for an ecosystem is continuous work. Fund Me Tools has no campaign end date because the work does not expire either.
