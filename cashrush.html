WHAT IT IS

A fast browser game

Inspired by the simple games people can play instantly — no accounts, no complicated onboarding, no long list of instructions.

Players chase scores. They unlock achievements. They return to beat their previous runs.

And somewhere between the obstacles, the achievements, and the progression, they may begin discovering a world connected to Bitcoin Cash.

No classroom.

No forced lesson.

No interruption saying “NOW YOU WILL LEARN ABOUT CRYPTO.”

The game comes first. Curiosity comes naturally. Bitcoin Cash is simply part of the world.

PHILOSOPHY

Simple games become daily habits

People return to games that feel good to replay. That return is the real opportunity — not a lecture inserted between levels.

Gameplay first

Fun is the product. Everything else is optional and secondary.

Achievement system

Scores, unlocks, reasons to come back for one more run.

Subtle discovery

Bitcoin Cash lives inside the world. Players who get curious can go deeper. No one is forced.

Future options

Room later for optional BCH and CashToken integrations — never required to play.
