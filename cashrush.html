// 07 CASHRUSH

CASHRUSH
Just one more run.

A fast browser game, the kind you can start instantly — no account, no onboarding, no long list of instructions. Chase a score. Beat your last run. Somewhere along the way, a world connected to Bitcoin Cash starts showing up around the edges.

Status: live beta Playable now Gameplay before education
Play CASHRUSH → Support this one

// The Philosophy

Play first. Discover the rest later.

Simple games become daily habits because they ask for almost nothing up front — thirty seconds, one tap, one run. CASHRUSH is built around that same instinct. No classroom. No forced lesson. No screen that interrupts the run to say "now you will learn about crypto." The game comes first. Curiosity comes on its own.

How it plays

  • Instant start, no accounts
  • Score-chasing runs built for replayability
  • An achievement system that rewards coming back

What's underneath

  • Bitcoin Cash is part of the world, not a lesson bolted onto it
  • Discovery happens between obstacles and achievements, never through interruption
  • Optional BCH and CashToken integrations are a future possibility, not a current feature
What exists today

CASHRUSH is live and playable right now at cashrush-play.vercel.app. It's a temporary Vercel domain — a permanent custom domain is one of the things support goes toward. It's still a beta: expect rough edges, and feedback genuinely shapes what gets fixed next.

What might come later

Optional BCH and CashToken integrations, once the core game itself is solid. Nothing here is promised on a timeline — it's a direction, not a roadmap.

// Support

It's playable. Now it needs a push.

CASHRUSH is past the concept stage — the run is done, the game is live. What it needs now is a custom domain instead of a temporary Vercel URL, real player feedback, and continued time to balance and polish. Play a run, tell me what breaks, or chip in toward the domain.
